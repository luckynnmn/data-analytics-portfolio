# Data Analytics Portfolio

A collection of data analytics projects demonstrating skills in Power BI dashboarding, DAX, Excel forecasting, financial analysis, and KPI/quality management across real-world business scenarios.

## Projects

### 📊 [Inventory Management](./Inventory-Management)
Built a Power BI dashboard tracking 5 inventory KPIs via DAX, identifying the root causes behind an 18.56% stockout rate through correlation analysis, and recommended ABC classification to rebalance stock without added investment.

### 📈 [Marketing Strategy: TikTok vs. Google](./Marketing-Strategy)
Compared 2020–2024 ad-revenue growth between TikTok and Google (CAGR 48% vs. 12.5%), built a 3-year Excel forecast, and linked algorithm design choices to measurable revenue outcomes.

### 💹 [NVIDIA vs. Intel: Financial Comparison](./NVIDIA-vs-Intel)
Analyzed 15 years (2010–2024) of financial data via WRDS/Compustat, contrasting NVIDIA's asset-light efficiency (91.9% ROE) with Intel's capital-intensive decline (−18.9% ROE).

### ✅ [Quality Management](./Quality-Management)
Owned accuracy/quality KPIs across 8 projects, raising average accuracy to 98.9% and achieving a Top 2 APAC ranking in 2024.

Power BI · DAX · Excel (Forecasting, Regression) · WRDS/Compustat · Root-Cause Analysis · KPI Management
