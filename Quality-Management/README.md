# Quality Management

## Overview
Owned quality and accuracy KPIs across 8 projects, driving measurable improvements in accuracy, consistency, and regional performance ranking.

## Key Results
- Raised average accuracy from **97.0% to 98.9%** (+1.84pp), hitting **100% quarterly target attainment** in Q1 and Q4 2024 against a 96% baseline.
- Ran root-cause analysis on KPI deviations, contributing to a **2.35% increase** in vertical performance and a **43.77% reduction** in recurring input errors vs. 2023.
- Achieved a **Top 2 APAC ranking** (2024); recognized as **APAC Top Performer, Q4 2024**.
