# NVIDIA vs. Intel: Financial Comparison

## Overview
A data-driven financial comparison of two S&P 500 companies — NVIDIA and Intel — examining 15 years of financial performance to evaluate their contrasting business models.

## Key Findings
- Extracted and analyzed **15 years (2010–2024)** of revenue, net income, and asset-efficiency data for both companies via WRDS/Compustat.
- Compared ROA/ROE trends, quantifying NVIDIA's asset-light efficiency (**65.3% ROA, 91.9% ROE in 2024**) against Intel's capital-intensive decline (**−9.6% ROA, −18.9% ROE**).
- Synthesized findings into a research report translating standardized accounting data into a cross-company investment comparison.

## Data Source
WRDS / Compustat
