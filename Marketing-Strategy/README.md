# Marketing Strategy: TikTok vs. Google Ad Revenue

## Overview
Compared personalized advertising strategies and growth trajectories between TikTok and Google by analyzing digital ad-revenue data from 2020–2024.

## Key Findings
- Analyzed 2020–2024 digital ad-revenue data — Google grew from **$146.9B to $264.6B**, TikTok from **$2.5B to $18.0B** — and calculated CAGR (**12.5% vs. 48%**) to compare platform growth trajectories.
- Built a 3-year revenue forecast using Excel linear regression (`FORECAST.LINEAR`), supported by a review of academic and industry literature on personalization.
- Connected platform algorithm design — **intent-based vs. engagement-based targeting** — to measurable revenue outcomes.

## Tools
Excel (FORECAST.LINEAR, regression analysis)
